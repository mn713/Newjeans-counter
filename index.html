About this project

This is a small personal, non-commercial automation project that posts a daily countdown video to TikTok, counting down the days until August 1, 2029. Each video is generated automatically once per day and shows the number of days remaining, overlaid on a looping background video.

The project consists of a Python video-generation script and a scheduled automation job that logs in via TikTok's Login Kit and publishes the daily video using the Content Posting API's Direct Post feature.

How it works

  1. A script calculates the number of days remaining and renders a short vertical video
  2. A scheduled job runs once every 24 hours
  3. The job authenticates with TikTok and publishes that day's video to a single, personally-owned TikTok account
  4. The automation stops itself automatically once the countdown reaches zero

Data & privacy

This app only ever accesses the developer's own TikTok account. It does not collect, store, or share data belonging to any other user. Full details are in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
